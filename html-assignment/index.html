About Me

Hello! I am Kat, an artist and international student at Algonquin College. I am half Greek-Cypriot and half Filipino, and have spent most of my childhood growing up in the Middle East in a British school, so my background is pretty diverse. Before taking IMD, I was in the Animation program for a bit before taking some time off to eventually decide on this current program instead. After my break back home, I only moved here to Canada on the 29th of August, so for the first two weeks of college, I was piled up with getting used to the classes and having to break my back with moving boxes... but at least now my apartment looks awesome :).

Outside of college, I have so many things that I like to do. I draw, animate, play games online, play instruments (and used to make music, though not currently), practice witchcraft/tarot reading, and much more that would make this paragraph even longer than it already is. The first thing people usually gather about me is that I am alternative, both visually and as a person - it is an integral part of my life. I have a huge love for alternative/gothic aesthetics and culture. I love gothic music and history, I love dark fashion, I collect crystals and I study tarot reading & many kinds of divination. In regards to my art, I can get super loaded with commissions for digital illustrations, 3D models made with Blender and Unity, or physical merchandise once in a while. I may have zero experience with web design and web building, but for my small communities in art and 3D modelling, I have my humble little carrd website that I like to share! Hopefully after this program, I can learn enough to be able to remake the entire thing from scratch.
Here are some of my hobbies that I spend the most time on:

  • Drawing (both digitally and traditionally)
  • Animating
  • 3D modelling on Blender and making them into calibrated models on Unity
  • Discovering new music and artists
  • Sewing and upcycling my clothes
  • Studying witchcraft and astrology
  • Doing tarot readings for my friends
  • Gaming/voice acting video games with friends

Alongside other things, most people - especially new friends - are quick to learn the importance of music in my life. Every second of the day, I am wearing earphones and listening to something (except during class, of course). As someone with ASD, having the background noise is a big comfort, and in general I just love music!
