What HTML tags I have learnt so far?

<h1>text</h1>
header tag that can go from h1 to h6 in a hierarchy of importance
<em>text</em>
emphasis, which italicizes text
<meta charset="UTF-8">
defines the set of characters encoded on the site, utf-8 specifically can accomodate many languages/characters, and is one of the most commonly used
<nav>text</nav>
adds a navigation area on the webpage that can be used for links to other sections/pages/sites
<ul>text</ul>
ul meaning unordered list, having multiple <li>text</li> in between to give the bullet point list items
